About Me

Hello! I’m Venkateswaran S, an electronics engineer blending hardware design expertise with a background in biological sciences. I graduated with dual majors in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and Biological Sciences from BITS Pilani.

Currently, I’m a Hardware Engineer at Cenizas Labs, working on real-time power management systems. I’ve interned at NVIDIA and Nihon Communication Solutions, gaining hands-on experience in memory verification and network architectures.

Beyond work, I enjoy teaching, public speaking, and exploring art forms. I’ve received national recognition like the Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Ignite Award for innovative problem-solving. Let’s connect and collaborate on exciting projects!
